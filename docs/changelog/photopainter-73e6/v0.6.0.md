**Waveshare PhotoPainter 7.3": scenes with buttons and sound**

- The PhotoPainter plays paced scenes: pages an agent sends that change when you press a button or after a set time. A colour page takes about 30 seconds to draw; a press is heard at once when the scene gives it a sound.
- Scenes can control the sound's volume, loop a tune and stop it: an alarm that fades in slowly and that a press sends back to silence, for example.
- A scene that ends itself, or that you end by holding KEY for 5 seconds, gives the screen back to its slideshow.
- The board measures its own redraw time and the room it has for pages, and tells the server, which refuses a scene that would not fit.
- This is the PhotoPainter's first release under its own tag. Install it over the air from the Wignet owner page; if an update does not start, the board goes back to the previous version by itself.
